What we collect and why

1. The enquiry form

When you send the “Get in touch” form, we receive your name, your laundry or company name, your work e-mail address, the daily volume you enter (optional), the page you sent it from, and the time of sending.

  • Purpose: to answer your enquiry, arrange a demo and prepare an offer.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in following up on a business enquiry (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Retention: up to 2 years after our last contact with you. If we sign a contract, the data becomes part of the customer relationship and is kept as long as the contract and the related legal retention periods require (for example 8 years for accounting records under Hungarian law).

2. E-mails you send us

If you write to us directly, we process your e-mail address, your name and whatever you include in your message, for the same purposes, on the same legal basis and for the same period as form enquiries.

3. Google Ads measurement (only with your consent)

We use the Google tag to see whether our Google Ads lead to enquiries. When you click Accept in the cookie banner, Google may set cookies on your device (for example _gcl_au, kept for up to 90 days) and record that you visited the site or sent the form after clicking one of our ads.

  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of the laundsoft.com home page; withdrawing does not affect processing that happened before.
  • If you click Reject or do nothing: no advertising cookies are set. Google's Consent Mode may still send cookieless signals (such as the time of the visit, the browser type and the fact that consent was not given), which Google uses only in aggregated form to estimate ad performance.

4. Your cookie choice

We remember whether you accepted or rejected cookies in your browser's local storage (ls_consent), so we do not ask again on every visit. If you pick a language with the language switcher, we remember that too (ls_lang), so the site opens in your language next time. Both are strictly necessary to respect your choices, are never sent to us or anyone else, and stay until you clear your browser data.

5. Technical data when you open the site

Like any website, opening laundsoft.com sends your IP address and basic browser information to the servers that deliver it: GitHub, which hosts the site, and Google Fonts, which delivers the typefaces. They use this data to deliver the content and to keep their services secure. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy (access);
  • correct inaccurate data (rectification);
  • delete your data (erasure);
  • restrict how we use it (restriction);
  • hand over the data you gave us in a machine-readable format (portability);
  • stop processing based on our legitimate interest (objection);
  • withdraw your consent to Google Ads cookies at any time (“Cookie settings” in the footer).

Write to mate.kiraly@neatly.eu. We answer within one month; if a request is complex, we may extend this by up to two more months and will tell you why.
